## 1. Executive Summary

This audit verifies that the **Indian PII Robustness Benchmark** codebase, canonical datasets, baseline predictions, and technical reports contain **zero real personally identifiable information (PII)**, zero proprietary credentials, zero leaked secrets, and zero restricted private identifiers.

All evaluation instances and adaptation samples were generated through deterministic and pseudorandom synthetic generation scripts conforming to formal privacy guarantees and the provisions of the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

## 2. Privacy & Synthetic Data Compliance Audit

### 2.1 Complete Synthetic Provenance
- **Audit Verification**:
  - All 964 canonical evaluation records (`data/raw/base_instances.jsonl`, `data/generated/language_realizations.jsonl`, `data/generated/adversarial_realizations.jsonl`, `data/generated/hard_negative_realizations.jsonl`) are derived from rule-based synthetic generators.
  - Names were generated by sampling combinatorial lists of common Indian first names, surnames, and patronymics across regional demographics.
  - Alphanumeric identifiers (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Driving License, Voter ID) were generated using syntactically valid structure templates with pseudorandom numbers.
  - Verhoeff checksums on synthetic Aadhaar numbers were generated without binding to any real individual or government identity repository.
  - UPI IDs, emails, and phone numbers use reserved fictitious domains (e.g., `@example.com`, standard non-routable numbers, test sandbox handles).
- **Status**: **`VERIFIED — 100% SYNTHETIC`**

### 2.2 Re-identification Risk Assessment
- **Risk Level**: **`NEGLIGIBLE`**
- **Analysis**: Because entity values were generated combinatorially without reference to any real-world database or crawled private documents, statistical linkage or re-identification of any living person is impossible.
